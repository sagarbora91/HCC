Owner, 27 Oct 2026 (DEC-120): HCC's expense module was designed in HCC, not taken from SCC. Fitting the month lock to HCC's accounting day locks: the month lock is a second, month-wide gate on expense records (SCC's tax-feed lock). It never opens anything HCC keeps closed — a closed business day, a locked cash-book day (DEC-065) or an Accounting-locked day stay locked whatever the Owner override — and like an accounting lock it keeps a snapshot (the feed) and flags later changes (stale = the accounting "drift" idea).

### HCC server gaps fixed (found by the SCC review)
1. A category must be an **active row of the Expense categories master** (unknown text refused, retired → MASTER_RETIRED); `/expenses/options` lists the master (was a hard-coded list).
2. The category's **"needs a bill"** flag is enforced (bill number required to submit; a draft may wait).
3. The category's **approval limit** replaces a limited approver's own limit (prototype `mnyApprovalLimit`).
4. **Retired vendors** are refused.
5. Maximum **₹10 crore** on both sides (the prototype stopped at ₹10 lakh).

### Kept as HCC (DEC-120)
Counted only once approved (every SCC total above reads approved records: budgets, GST paid, petty disbursed, month feed, month CSV); Manager ≤ ₹5,000 or the category limit; never your own nor one you changed; the Cashier never reverses or voids; day close blocked by pending expenses; the cash drawer moves on approval only.

## Questions for the owner
1. **Payroll into expenses (SCC feed F01)** — not posted: HCC's payroll journal already books salaries; SCC's feed would count them twice. Keep it out?
2. **Cashier rules** — denominations (SCC ₹2000…₹1 + deposit grid), the ₹1 "balanced" tolerance, close without difference approval, day approval by role: unchanged (not part of DEC-120). Keep HCC's?
3. **Counting** — SCC counts a pending entry at once (bug B1); HCC counts after approval (DEC-120), so budgets, GST paid, petty balance and the month feed show approved figures only; the add form shows "would be n % once approved". Right?
4. **SCC's 100 % inconsistency (B9)** — exactly 100 % is "near limit" on the budget table but an error alert. Ported as SCC. Align (e.g. "over" from 100 %)?
5. **Who sets budgets and recurring templates** — SCC: anyone (budgets), Accounts / Owner (templates). HCC: Manager, Accountant, Owner for both. Owner only (DEC-058)?
6. **Petty cash** — SCC lets any role set the float without approval and posts disbursements without approval or a closed-day check (B3). HCC: a top-up is approved by a second person (Manager ≤ ₹5,000); a disbursement is a drawer-cash expense for approval, dated today. A disbursement may exceed the float (SCC has no check; the status then says "Replenish soon"). Keep?
7. **Udhaar cash** — as SCC, a payment is recorded against the credit only and never reaches the drawer or income. A cashier who puts that cash in the drawer will count over. SCC tells users to add it separately as income; HCC has no manual income entry. How should the cash be taken (a desk receipt)? Keep the ₹0.50 overpay tolerance (B10)? The reminder names the hotel only — add the town?
8. **Write-off of a credit** — SCC: anyone; HCC: Manager, Accountant, Owner. Right?
9. **Month lock** — SCC has no unlock (B12) and asks a step-up re-auth for the override; HCC has neither (the signed-in Owner + a reason). Add an Owner unlock with a reason, like Accounting's? In HCC approvals and reversals dated in a locked month also need the override (SCC's approval has no check). Should generating the feed also lock the month's closed days in Accounting?
10. **Chosen date** — HCC takes the business date or the Owner-reopened day only (every earlier HCC day is closed by the day close); drawer cash only on the business date. Future dates refused (SCC allowed them, B5). A draft or pending expense whose day has closed since is dated the business date when submitted / decided (HCC's earlier rule). Keep?
11. **Void** — SCC lets the cashier void (B6); HCC: pending only by Owner / Manager / Accountant (DEC-063 G5); the maker may void their own draft. SCC's reason is always met by the default first option (B11) — require a typed note?
12. **Edit** — new safeguard: whoever changes someone else's pending expense may not approve it (nobody approves their own). SCC has no such rule. Keep?
13. **Vendor list** — new names are now added on save by any expense user (SCC auto-add), though the list was Owner-only (DEC-MA-3.1). The Owner still edits / retires rows; retired vendors are refused. Right? (SCC's soft GSTIN check, any-role vendor edit and delete are not taken: the list stays a money master.)
14. **GST on expenses in Accounting** — the voucher still books the whole bill to the category; input GST is not split to a GST-input ledger. CA to decide. GST rate is capped at 100 % (SCC has no cap).
15. **Recurring post** — raises a Draft the poster submits (DEC-120 brief); SCC posts it straight as pending. A voided / rejected / reversed post makes the template due again. Keep?
16. **CSV** — SCC's columns in SCC order plus HCC `Ref` and `Status`; Firm = the hotel's name, Store = "Unassigned" (one shared drawer), CreatedBy = the user's name (SCC: the role). The ledger file lists every record (drafts, rejected, void with their status). Right?
