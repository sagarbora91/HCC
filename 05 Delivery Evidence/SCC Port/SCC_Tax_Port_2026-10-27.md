**What the ≈₹ figures use** (SCC reads its shell's bridge feed, not in the inputs; HCC computes from its own data, money in paise, shown in whole rupees as SCC):
- **EPF** = employee PF + employer PF, **ESIC** = employee ESIC + employer ESIC, of every line of the month's **locked** pay run (`hr.pay_run` / `pay_run_line`; prototype `staffX.runs`); a draft run counts nothing.
- **Professional Tax** = PT deducted in that locked run.
- **GST (estimate)** = CGST + SGST + IGST on the tax invoices issued in the month (rooms and restaurant), less the month's credit notes — the GSTR-1 working summary's SAC total (`ivc.invoice_line` − `ivc.credit_note_line`; prototype `gstr1Summary`). Input tax credit is not deducted. (SCC's estimate was income × category rate from its Expense module.)

## HCC rules removed / kept (brief)

- **Kept as HCC extras** (owner questions 12): the "Due this week" dashboard card and assistant answer (`dueSoonDays`), the simulated CA pack with CA name / e-mail, the "N day(s) after the due date" audit note (from the user's filed-on date), a 5 MB evidence-file limit and text-length limits.
