## HCC rules removed (SCC has none)
Carry-forward (fields kept at 0 for history, `CK_leave_type_no_carry`), EL encashment flag and `FnfRules.ElEncashment` (F&F encashment stays a manual adjustment), the 15-day maximum, no crossing 31 March, the mandatory reason (3+ characters), the per-department **blocking** cap (default 2), the daily-wage "unpaid types only" rule, the "at least one unpaid type" list rule, skipping the person's weekly off and paid holidays when counting leave, whole-request refusal on overlap.

## HCC safeguards kept (DEC-119, DEC-058, DEC-075 and platform rules)
Nobody approves their own leave or their own request (FORBIDDEN, `CK_leave_not_self`); removing leave = cancel with an audit record, never a delete; role permissions (own leave or PERM-HR-002 for anyone, PERM-HR-003 decides, leave settings / overrides / blackouts / import / clear Owner only — PERM-HR-009, agenda items managers); staff keyed by ID; no leave before the business date — past days go through DEC-075 attendance corrections (which now use the leave week-off set and per-staff entitlement); inactive staff refused; attendance on the date blocks an approval and approved full-day leave blocks marking attendance; F-15 "N staff already over the new entitlement" warning; every mutation through ICommandRunner (audit + outbox); server dates local `DateOnly`.

## Old data
- **Prototype**: `restoreState` migrates an old save (leave spans) to one record per date it counted when stored (the old rule: the person's rostered working days that were not paid holidays), so balances and pay stay as they were; the first date keeps the id (`HR-LV-1`, then `HR-LV-1-2` …); a span that counted no date keeps its first date (nothing is dropped); idempotent; a stored Leave types list gets "Other". No SCHEMA bump (new fields optional).
- **Server**: migration **4110** splits `hr.leave_request` spans the same way in SQL (roster cell, else weekly off unless the pattern leaves it to the roster; active paid holidays; weekday by `DATEDIFF` from a known Sunday so `DATEFIRST` does not matter), widens the settings to decimals, makes department caps advisory INT, adds OTH, clears carry / encashment, creates `hr.leave_ent_override`, `hr.leave_blackout`, `hr.leave_agenda`. Upgrade test on an old database: every record kept, each request's day units unchanged.
- Payroll figures: BASE and FY26 stored and computed pay runs, Apr–Oct 2026, are **identical** before and after (checked line by line); the golden August / September lines did not change.

## Questions for the owner
Built as SCC unless the line says otherwise.
1. **Q1 Week-off default.** SCC's default is Saturday + Sunday (leave on those days is silently skipped). HCC is built with **no day ticked** (a 7-day hotel) — the Owner can tick days in Leave settings. Keep "none"? The per-staff weekly off stays for the roster only.
2. **Q2 MAX_SHIFT 4 never applies** — MAX_DAY 3 binds first; the day view shows "u / 4" bars. Kept both as SCC.
3. **Q3 Store holidays warn** ("leave is discouraged") although SCC calls them "marker only". Ported as SCC.
4. **Q4 Balance ignores pending leave and approval re-checks nothing**: several pending requests can together exceed the quota and all be approved; the over-quota warning is confirmed by whoever records the leave. Ported as SCC (HCC keeps only the attendance check at approval).
5. **Q5 Requested days include dates later skipped** (duplicate / full), so the EXCEEDS warning can be false. Ported as SCC.
6. **Q6 Decisions**: SCC lets anyone decide (also their own) and records the nominated approver as decider. HCC keeps DEC-119 (nobody decides their own leave or request) and records the person who decided; the chosen approver is stored and shown separately. Recording leave for others and changing settings stay role-based (DEC-058).
7. **Q7 Remove** is a hard delete in SCC; HCC cancels with an audit record (DEC-119). SCC's past-view guard looks at the viewed date; HCC also refuses new leave before the business date (DEC-075 corrections). Clear All cancels instead of deleting and keeps locked pay months.
8. **Q8 Import is stricter than the screen** (a second record of the same person on a date is skipped even AM + PM). Ported as SCC.
9. **Q9 Backup omits caps and blackouts; Clear All keeps them** while saying "All data cleared". Ported as SCC.
10. **Q10 Staff by ID**, not by name (SCC merges same-name staff). Kept HCC (DEC-119).
11. **Q11 The 7-day strip counts records, not people** (AM + PM of one person = 2 on leave). Ported as SCC.
12. **Q12 A request across 31 March** is checked against the start date's FY only. Ported as SCC (usage is then booked by date).
13. **Q13 The calendar cannot go before May 2026**, which hides FY26 history from April 2026 on the calendar (the report and payroll still have it). Ported as SCC.
14. **Q14 The approvals note "decide within 7 days"** belongs to SCC's shell sync; HCC reads leave directly — not ported; please confirm.
15. **Q15 A cleared entitlement saves 0**; fractions allowed, no maximum. Ported as SCC. (The Master configuration hub still edits whole days only; the Leave settings screen and CSV import take halves, up to 2 decimals.)
16. **Q16 SCC's date keys come from UTC** (right only at or east of UTC, e.g. IST). The server uses local `DateOnly`; the prototype uses date strings.
17. **Q17 "Store" = department**: SCC's per-store advisory caps (default 3), blackout scope and "Short:" use HCC departments; the blocking department cap is gone. Confirm.
18. **Q18 Approvers = the Owner + enabled Manager-role users** (SCC: Owner + Store Managers). Confirm.
19. **Other HCC items kept, to confirm**: inactive staff refused; attendance blocks approval and approved full-day leave blocks marking attendance; settings Owner-only, agenda items managers; the approver cannot be the person on leave; F-15 "already over the new entitlement" warning; a DEC-075 correction to leave still refuses when the balance is short (no SCC confirm there); a request has no length limit (SCC's loop) but the balance and blackout checks look at the first 400 days (SCC's guard); leave on a rostered weekly off or holiday now counts as leave in pay (SCC feed); daily-wage staff may take any category (each is a paid leave day except LOP); F&F encashment is a manual adjustment.
