## DEC-130 — Udhaar cash goes into the drawer
- **Desk receipt:** an Udhaar payment takes a front-desk payment method (Payment methods master; Vendor credit is refused). **Cash** goes into the open shift's drawer under the same rules as other drawer cash: the business day and cash-book day open, an open shift on the till for the business date, the payments permission on that shift (server PERM-PAY-001 with the shift scope — Reception / Cashier on their own shift; the Accountant cannot take cash). Without an open shift: "Open cash shift required: Udhaar cash goes into the open shift's drawer …". **UPI / card / bank** go to their own ledgers and need the transaction reference like other receipts. The ₹0.50 overpay tolerance stays.
- **Drawer:** server `pay.cash_movement` kind `payment`, source type `udhaar` (source = the settlement) through `ICashDrawerPort`; prototype `udhaar-desk.ts` feeds `drawer()`, `shiftMovements` and the report's cash-in. So the cashier's expected cash, the shift count, the day close and the cash book include it (tests: expected cash, count closes with no difference, closing record, cash-book line).
- **Accounting:** HCC never booked an Udhaar credit (the register keeps it, as SCC: "outstanding credit can never inflate income"). The payment therefore **settles a receivable, never income**: a Receipt voucher Dr the tender · Cr **1120 Udhaar receivables (customer credit)** (new ledger, mapping key `receipt:udhaar`, sub `udhaar-payment` so the folio collections check is untouched). Server event `UdhaarPaymentRecorded`; prototype voucher derived the same way. Because the credit itself is not booked, 1120 shows a credit balance equal to what was received — see the owner / CA question below.
- **Voiding a payment:** not supported (SCC has none), so there is no drawer reversal to build; writing off a credit (`EXP_UD_VOID`) still moves no cash. A closed day cannot take a payment. Payments recorded before DEC-130 (no desk flag) stay out of the drawer and the books.
- **Screens:** the payment dialog says the cash goes into the drawer (expected cash, day close, cash book), asks the reference for non-cash modes, and refuses on the device without an open shift; production client sends the till with the open shift and reloads the cash desk.

## DEC-131 — Owner unlock of an expense month
- `EXP_MONTH_UNLOCK` (prototype) / `UnlockExpenseMonth` (server, `POST /commands/UnlockExpenseMonth`, production client mapped): Owner only, a reason of 3–200 characters (like Accounting's period unlock), audited `EXPENSE_MONTH_UNLOCK`, every unlock kept (`ops.expense_month_unlock`; prototype `unlocks`). After the unlock entries dated in the month need no Owner override (and record none); the feed is marked stale; "Lock again" (`LockExpenseMonth`) regenerates the feed, clears stale and keeps the unlock history. Refused: not locked / already unlocked (INVALID_TRANSITION), short reason (VALIDATION), anyone but the Owner (FORBIDDEN).
- **Screens:** Month & tax section — "Unlock month…" (Owner, locked month) with a reason prompt, the unlocked banner and history; desktop and phone share the section and dialog.

## DEC-135 — input GST: ITC allowed / disallowed
- Every expense with GST on the bill carries **ITC allowed / disallowed**, chosen on the add form and editable while the expense can be edited (Draft / Pending; the change is logged when "allowed" is set or cleared). **Default: disallowed.** Never allowed on more GST than the bill amount. A reversal record mirrors the original (−GST, same flag). Old expenses = disallowed (prototype restore migration `expMigrateItc` by shape; server column `itc_allowed` default 0, check: only with GST).
- **Accounting:** ITC allowed → Dr expense category (amount − GST) · Dr **1300 GST input credit** · Cr cash / bank (amount); disallowed → as before (whole bill to the category). New mapping key `tax:gst-input` (group "GST input credit (ITC)") appears in the mapping screen and can be remapped (Set / Reset ledger mapping, history); the Tally XML names the ledger "GST input credit". One ledger — no CGST / SGST / IGST split (the CA can ask for it later). Events published before carry no fields and book as before.
- **Other figures:** budgets keep counting the full bill; "GST paid on purchases" shows ITC allowed and ITC disallowed separately (Month & tax KPIs; the month feed stores the allowed part); the ledger and month CSVs gain an **ITC** column after Status.
- **Recurring templates and petty-cash payouts** carry no GST fields, so they carry no ITC flag; a recurring draft gets GST + ITC through Edit before it is submitted.
