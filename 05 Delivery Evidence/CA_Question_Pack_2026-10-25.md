# Hotel Control Centre (HCC): Questions for the Chartered Accountant

Prepared 25 October 2026; revised 26 October 2026 (corrections from the independent audit, owner decisions of 26 October, and a new last column on every table). Hotel in Maharashtra, intra-state supplies.

**About the app.** Hotel Control Centre is the hotel's operations software: bookings, room and restaurant billing, cash book, staff attendance, leave, payroll and a compliance calendar. A prototype and a production server exist; the app is not yet in use at the hotel and all data in it is fictional. The starting rates, limits and due dates were taken from public sources and from the owner's earlier payroll and tax tool, and each is marked **"Illustrative — CA to confirm"**. The app calculates and reminds. **Nothing is filed, paid or e-mailed automatically**: returns, challans and the bank salary file are produced or tracked for a person to act on.

**Which answers need a developer.** An independent code audit (26 Oct 2026) found that the **rates, limits, slab amounts and due dates are settings** the owner can change, but many **rules are written in the program**: for example what counts as PF and ESIC wages, the overtime base, the 26-day divisor for daily wages, the 15-minute late grace, the settlement limits, the SAC codes, the B2CL limit and the chart of accounts. If your answer to one of those differs from today's rule, a developer must change the program before the app follows it. The last column of every table says which case applies.

## How to read the last column

- **Setting** = the owner changes it in Master configuration (Owner only), no developer. The key or screen is named. Every change is dated and audited; earlier periods keep the old rule.
  - GST (Master configuration, Money and tax, GST): starts on the business date or later, up to two years ahead; **cannot be back-dated**. Nights and bills already posted keep the rate they were posted at; a past mistake is corrected by credit note, not by changing the rate. A past date already in the list (for example 22 Sep 2025) cannot be edited on the screen.
  - Payroll rates (Master configuration, People, payroll and leave, Statutory rates): start on the 1st of a month after the last locked pay-run; locked months never change.
  - Due dates (Compliance calendar, Manage obligations): start on a date chosen (today or later); earlier periods keep their date.
- **Needs code** = a developer must change the program. We name the code location from the audit (`08 Production/src/…`, line numbers as audited on 26 Oct 2026) and give a rough developer-hour estimate where the audit gave one; "no estimate" means the audit gave none. Where one question has both a rule part and a rate part, both are shown.
- Keys written like `gst.…`, `hr.…`, `cmp.…` are the names shown on the Master configuration screen; names in brackets, such as `pfEeBps`, are the matching fields in the server.
- If the owner would rather have every payroll constant turned into a dated setting, the audit puts that at about 12 developer hours (A04-03). Tell us which ones you want.

## How to reply

Answer inline, or mark each row **Confirm** or **Change to ...** with the correct rate, rule or date and the date from which it applies. What happens next depends on the last column. A **Setting** change is entered by the owner in Master configuration (or the Compliance calendar) as a dated change; no developer is needed. A **Needs code** change goes to the developers as a separate request after your reply, with the hours shown; nothing in the app changes until that work is done and tested. A **Confirm** on a rule marked Needs code means no work. If a new rate or rule must apply from a past date, please say so: settings cannot be back-dated, so earlier bills and pay-runs have to be corrected separately (credit notes, adjustments).
