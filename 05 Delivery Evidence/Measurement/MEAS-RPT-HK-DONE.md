Each report is hidden and refuses `MEAS_OFF` through the existing engine hook (`MEAS_REPORT_KEY` / `IMeasuredReport`); the six keys already map correctly (`meas.hk.productivity / .ooo / .ppm / .utilities / .linen / .assets`), nothing to fix.
- rpt.rooms.hk-productivity (P1, D-S max 31 days, PERM-HK-001): per attendant and shift (by start hour). Red: pass rate < 95 %; median departure room > 45 min; same-day re-let not passed before arrival.
- rpt.rooms.ooo-days (D-B max 92): room-days from the room out-of-order window / block, captured reason and work-order link, lost revenue = room-days x `meas.target.adr`. Red: > 7 days without a work order; sellable room idle >= 14 nights.
- rpt.rooms.ppm-compliance (D-F max 366): per asset kind. Red: preventive overdue > 7 days; P1 MTTR > 24 h; 3 failures of one asset within 90 days.
- rpt.rooms.utilities-per-room (D-M): per day from monotonic meter readings (use counted on the later reading's day; LPG cylinders x 19 kg), per occupied room-night, month row with unit cost, cost per night and last year. Red: > 15 % above the 3-month average per occupied night.
- rpt.stock.linen-cost (D-M, PERM-STK-001): par, on hand, batches, lost, damaged, captured discards with reason, issued, cost per occupied night. Red: loss > `meas.target.lossPct` (2 %) of par; cost per night above `meas.target.linenCost` when the Owner has set one.
- rpt.acc.asset-life (D-ASOF, PERM-ACC-001): MNT assets and fixed-asset register rows, age, expected life, grade, replacement due, WDV (= register cost), replacement cost; FY row with FF&E reserve accrued (`ffe.reservePct` x FY room revenue) and capex spent. Red: past expected life with no active plan; capex > accrued.

Money flags changed against the catalogue text (flag "n" / "Money columns: none" contradicts the "behind PERM-RPT-002 / MNT-004 / STK-004" cost notes): ooo-days, utilities-per-room, linen-cost are `moneyFlag` y with the money columns `lostRevenue`, `unitCost` + `costPerNight`, `costPerNight` respectively (gates PERM-RPT-002, PERM-RPT-002, PERM-STK-004). asset-life money columns: wdv, replacementCost, reserveAccrued, capexSpent.
