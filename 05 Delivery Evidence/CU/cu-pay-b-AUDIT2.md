## Findings

1. **FIX - the hall cash-receipt limit (statutory) now misses a renamed Cash row.** `08 Production/src/Hcc.Infrastructure.SqlServer/Accounting/SqlFlowsStore.cs:66` (`CashParty.Hall`): the event's earlier cash receipts are summed with `r.tender = 'Cash'`. The limit is rulebook B9 / DEC-260: the Owner's "Cash receipt limit" (seed ₹2,00,000 per person per day or per booking / transaction / **event**, Income-tax Act 2025 s.186, old s.269ST; `CashGuardService.cs:56-57`), refused at or above the limit. **Before this fix round** the server hall path accepted only the fixed list and stored the literal `Cash` for every drawer receipt, so the sum was complete (a renamed Cash could not be taken on the server at all). **This fix round** stores the current master name (`HallBillingCommands.TenderAsync`, line 131), so after the Owner renames Cash ("Front till"), or adds a second till method, every new cash receipt is left out of the sum. Example: ₹1,50,000 "Front till" advance, then a further ₹1,00,000 "Front till" advance on the same event: the second sees ₹0 earlier cash and is accepted (₹2,50,000 cash for one event). Mixed history (old `Cash` rows plus new `Front till` rows) is also under-counted. The fix round therefore **opened** this hole; the DONE file lists it as a follow-up, but it is a direct consequence of the snapshot change and must be fixed in this lane.
   Right: count every ADVANCE / BALANCE receipt of the event that went into a till, not a label. Simplest and exact: `AND EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM pay.cash_movement m WHERE m.source_type = 'hall' AND m.source_id = r.receipt_id)` instead of `r.tender = 'Cash'` (hall cash always gets one movement with `source_id = receipt_id`, `CashierService.HallSource`; `UQ_cash_movement_source (source_type, source_id)` serves the lookup; it is the hall twin of the folio branch's `shift_id IS NOT NULL`; the receipt being checked is excluded as before and has no movement yet anyway). Alternative: resolve `tender` through `mst.payment_method` joined to a till `mst.bank_account`, including former names from `mst.master_history`; more code, same result. Test in `Hcc.Hall.Tests` (pattern of `HallIntegrationSqlTests.Hall_cash_receipts_stop_at_the_cash_limit_per_event`): rename Cash to "Front till"; accept with a ₹1,50,000 "Front till" advance; a further ₹50,000 by "Cash" (earlier name) **and** by "Front till" are both refused (`VALIDATION`, CASH_LIMIT); a ₹50,000 UPI advance still passes; one old literal-`Cash` row plus a `Front till` row are counted together. The test must fail on cb127759.
