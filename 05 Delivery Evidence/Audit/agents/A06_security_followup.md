## (d) What a Tech admin can still do while F-M03 stays open (plain English)

The Tech admin's own powers today are: create and manage staff accounts for the non-money roles (housekeeper, supervisor, maintenance), register and revoke tablets, view (not change money) configuration, see the health/backup page, upload and preview master-data files, and see their own pay. The S2 fix closed the serious hole: a Tech admin can no longer reset the password or PIN of, or switch off, any Owner, Manager, Reception, Cashier, Accountant or other Tech admin account, and cannot give anyone a money role (the server refuses, and the tests pin it). What they can still do is this. They can reset a housekeeper's, supervisor's or maintenance worker's PIN, register a phone as a hotel device, and sign in **as that person** — seeing that person's tasks and that person's own pay, and completing or reporting work in their name; because nothing forces the staff member to change the PIN afterwards, the Tech admin keeps a working PIN until the Owner notices the `CREDENTIAL_SET` row in the audit log (which the Tech admin cannot read or delete) or the staff member changes it. They cannot see any hotel money, bookings, guest contacts or ID numbers through such an account, because those roles never hold those permissions. They can also revoke any tablet, which logs every PIN user off that tablet until an Owner or Tech admin registers it again — a nuisance, not a theft. The same "shared secret" gap applies to the Owner: when the Owner resets an Accountant's password, both know it until the Accountant changes it, which weakens who-did-what evidence for that account. Closing F-M03 fully means a "must change at next sign-in" flag plus a screen (about a day's work, A06-01), and the Owner should treat `CREDENTIAL_SET` entries in Approvals & audit as something to check weekly until then.
