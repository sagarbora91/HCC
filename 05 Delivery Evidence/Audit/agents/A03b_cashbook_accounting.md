- **The accounting bugs are about coverage, not arithmetic.** The accounting projector does not book several money events, and a daily cash task is missing:
  - nothing on the server can move cash out of the drawer to the bank, to the owner, or to another till;
  - group master folio receipts, group attrition and staff advances never reach the ledgers.
- **The projector cannot post anything twice.** Three things protect this: a dedup row per event, a unique source key, and append-only triggers on the source tables.
- **One bad event stops all accounting.** If one event keeps failing, every later event waits behind it forever. Nobody is told, and there is no dead-letter.
- **No money can be back-dated after a day is closed.** Corrections are posted as dated adjustments in the open business day, which is the "adjustment-only" option. DEC-028 is still marked "Needs owner decision" in the register even though the code already works this way.

## Projector idempotency and outage analysis
- **Delivery.** `AccountingProjectorWorker` polls every 2 s and calls `OutboxConsumer.ConsumeAsync` (100 events per batch, in outbox order). Each event gets its own transaction: lock and check `plat.consumer_dedup`, insert the dedup row, apply the effect, commit. Redelivery finds the row and returns false. Deadlocks (1205) are retried 5 times.
- **Effect.** One source per operational record. The key is `charge:{id}`, `receipt:{id}`, `pos:{bill}`, `pos-void:{bill}`, `expense:{id}`, `shift:{id}/cash-difference`, `payroll:{run}[/lockN]`, `payroll-paid:…` or `payroll-unlock:…`. It is inserted only if absent, under UPDLOCK + HOLDLOCK, and backed by `UQ_acc_source`. Sources and entries are append-only by trigger. A re-published event for the same record (a new outbox row) is ignored; the test `Consumer_is_exactly_once…` checks both paths. Verdict: **idempotent**.
- **Cash differences.** P4 publishes no `ShiftDifferenceDecided` event, so accepted differences are swept from `pay.shift` with the same key. Both paths cannot post. The sweep runs after each consume and is counted in `PendingSql`.
- **Projector down at day close.** The close does not need accounting. Its checks and snapshot read the operational tables (`ClosingService.CloseAndSnapshotAsync`). The day closes, and the next day opens, normally. Accounting for that date stays "live". `LockAccountingPeriod` refuses while `PendingEventsAsync > 0`, which counts every unconsumed event for the property, not just that date. Once the worker catches up, the sources arrive dated with the original business date (the event payload's date), so a later lock is complete. Late sources cannot land in an already locked date. A date can only be locked after the business date has moved past it, all writers dated D committed before the D barrier, and the pending check covers events not yet projected. That is why "drift" can only come from tests.
- **The weak points** are A03b-04 (one bad event stops everything, silently) and A03b-05 (exports and the trial balance during a lag or outage carry no "provisional or incomplete" flag).

## Shared till / drawer rules (DEC-052, DEC-035, DEC-065)
- **Expected cash.** Expected = opening float + Σ signed movements (`CashDrawer.Expected`). The parts are checked against the direct sum, and the code throws if they disagree. Folio cash moves only through RecordReceipt and ExecuteAdjustment. POS sale, POS void, expense and expense reversal go through `RecordMovementAsync`, which checks the sign and needs an open shift on that till dated the current business date.
- **POS cash on the shared drawer.** An outlet cash bill adds one `pos-sale` movement and one POS Sales voucher (Dr 1000). A room-posted bill adds no cash and is booked once, from its folio `ChargePosted`; the projector skips `mode = folio` (`AccountingVoucherProjector.cs:129-134`). A void is allowed only for outlet bills on the same day: a negative movement and a negative Sales voucher.
- **Refund rule.** Reception and Cashier only *request* a refund (PERM-PAY-003 'R'). Approval needs PERM-APR-001 (Manager ≤ ₹1,000, Owner unlimited) from someone other than the requester. Execution needs PERM-PAY-004 (Reception and Cashier on their **own** shift 'S'; Manager and Owner any shift) by someone other than the requester (DEC-031). A cash refund leaves the till chosen at execution, which must have an open shift for the current date. The refund is capped at the receipt's remainder, under a lock on the receipt row. A departed, settled stay needs a credit note first.
- **Count sheet.** Notes 500/200/100/50/20/10 plus coins in paise. The grid total must equal the counted amount. If it differs from expected, the counter must give a reason, and an approval is raised (shift `Submitted`, no cash can move).
- **Variance posting.** The difference must be accepted by PERM-SHF-002: Owner and Accountant unlimited, Manager ≤ ₹200. The person who submitted the count cannot accept it (PERM-SOD-004). The movements must not have changed since the count, otherwise the result is STALE. Once accepted, the shift closes and the next opening float = counted (DEC-035). Accounting books a Journal: Dr 5900 / Cr 1000 when short, the reverse when over. A rejected difference reopens the shift for a recount.
- **Cash-book lock (DEC-065).** PERM-CLS-003 (Owner, Manager, Accountant). It needs a closed day. The counter, the person who accepted the difference and the closer cannot lock. Only the Owner can reopen, with a reason. All of this is audited.
